# BS 2083 calendar verification

Verified on 2026-09-20 against independently published calendar grids.
This is publisher verification, not government certification. Source provenance
is retained privately outside the repository.

## Published boundaries

Month lengths were transcribed from calendar grids, not generated by our
converter. Gregorian years/months were interpreted from selected month context
and adjacent-month transitions.

The corrected lengths are `[31,31,32,31,31,31,30,29,30,29,30,30]` (365 days).
The active encoded value at index `2083 - 1970 = 113` changes from `5527226`
to `5315258`, using the existing two-bits-per-month encoding (`length - 29`).

## Scope and impact

Four months change: Asoj 30 → 31, Mangsir 30 → 29, Poush 29 → 30,
and Magh 30 → 29. The year total does not change. Gregorian mappings before
2083 and from 2084 onward are unchanged; only internal 2083 boundaries change.
The retained reference row already matches this published 2083 table.

A subsequent month-by-month audit of BS 2078–2086 found all 108 published
month lengths matched the corrected active table, with no missing pages or
parse errors. Raw pages and audit results are retained outside the repository.
This establishes agreement with one publisher, not official approval of future
years or independent verification of every Gregorian conversion.

The active/reference discrepancies for 2084–2086 remain, but the active rows
match the audited publisher data. No changes were made to those years.

## Regression coverage

`DateConverterTests.testPublished2083MonthBoundaries` checks each published
month length, its first and last day in both directions, invalid days, and the
2084 New Year boundary. Expected dates are independent of the active table.
`testAsoj2083Boundary` explicitly covers October 17 → Asoj 31 and October 18 →
Kartik 1. `CalendarViewModelTests.testAsoj2083CalendarIncludesDay31` covers the
reported grid behavior. Release CI runs the suite before packaging.

The full-range navigation test checks internal consistency, not independently
verified accuracy. Before each Nepali new year, compare the upcoming year with
its published calendar and update source-backed fixtures when needed.
